## 1. Joining

1.1 You apply on our website and confirm your email address. We review every application and may accept or decline it at our discretion. You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract under Nigerian law.

1.2 One ambassador account per person, email address and phone number. Your sign-in is personal: do not share it.

1.3 You give us accurate details, including a bank account **in your own name** for payments, and keep them up to date. A change of bank details is confirmed by a code sent to your email.

## 2. Referrals

2.1 We give you a referral code and link. A school is referred by you when it signs up using your code or link (we remember a link for [PLACEHOLDER: 90] days on the device it was opened on), or when we record you as its ambassador.

2.2 Each school has one ambassador: the first valid code used when it signs up. We decide disputes about who referred a school, and we may correct a school's ambassador where a mistake was made.

2.3 You may not refer a school you own, run or work for, or one where you are the administrator, contact or a user ("self-referral"). Such sign-ups earn nothing.
