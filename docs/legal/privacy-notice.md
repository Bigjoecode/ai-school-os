This notice explains how personal information about **students, parents and guardians, and school staff** is used in AI School OS, the school management and learning platform your school uses. It is written in plain English. It is meant to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the guidance of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## For children: the short version

> **Your school uses AI School OS to look after your school records.**
>
> - It keeps things like your name, class, attendance, homework and results, and health details the school needs to keep you safe.
> - Only people at your school who need to see your records can see them. Other students can never see your records.
> - If you use the AI tutor, it helps you learn. Your questions go to an AI company only to get you an answer. They are not used to train the AI.
> - Your teachers check the marks and comments the AI suggests about you.
> - You or your parent can ask the school to see your records, fix mistakes, or remove things that should not be kept.
> - If something worries you, talk to your teacher, your parent or the school office.

## 2. What information is used

**About students**

- Identity and school details: name, date of birth, gender, admission number, class and arm, house, photo, address, status (for example active or graduated).
- Health and welfare (sensitive information): blood group, genotype, allergies, chronic conditions, medical notes, sick-bay visits, authorised pick-up people, exeats.
- Learning: attendance, homework and hand-ins (which can include photos, videos or voice notes), scores, report cards, teacher remarks, behaviour and merit records, online exam answers, practice attempts, study plans, flashcards and topic mastery.
- AI tutor (where the school uses it): the student's questions and the tutor's answers, voice clips when the student chooses to speak, and short learning notes the tutor keeps (for example "finds fractions hard").
- Careers: interest-quiz answers and results, saved careers, planned subjects and courses, counsellor notes.
- Other school services where used: library loans, transport, hostel, live class attendance, certificates, ID cards, admission applications, alumni details.

**About parents and guardians**

- Name, relationship to the child, phone number, email, address, occupation.
- Fees: invoices, payments, receipts and discounts. Card details are handled by Paystack, not stored by the school or the platform.
- Messages sent to you (email, SMS, WhatsApp, in-app) and your messages to the school's WhatsApp assistant, if you use it.
- Your consent to this notice, and when you gave or withdrew it.

**About staff**

- Name, contact details, date of birth, gender, staff number, job title, department, qualifications, next of kin, employment dates.
- HR and payroll: attendance, leave, awards, salary grade, pay, deductions, bank details and payslips.
- Teaching work: lesson plans, timetables, marks entered and remarks written.

**About everyone who signs in**

- Sign-in account (email, name, phone), roles, last sign-in, whether two-step sign-in is on.
- Security records: an audit log of important actions (who did what and when, with IP address and browser), and sign-in sessions.
- Usage records: counts of requests and AI usage (for cost control and fair use).

## 4. AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence from outside providers. The full details are in **How we use AI with your children's data** (/legal/children). In short:

- Information is sent to an AI provider **only to answer the request** being made (for example a student's question to the tutor, or a teacher asking for a draft remark).
- **It is not used to train AI models.** The platform does not train its own models on your data, and according to the providers' published API terms, data sent through their APIs is not used to train their models by default.
- **People stay in charge.** Marks, remarks and other AI suggestions about a student are reviewed by staff before they count.
- **Children only see their own work.** The AI tutor can only use the signed-in student's own information.
- AI features the school has switched on are listed on the consent screen parents see.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you (or a parent, for a child) can ask the school to:

- **see** the information held about you and get a copy;
- **correct** information that is wrong or incomplete;
- **delete** information that should no longer be kept;
- **restrict** or **object to** some uses of it;
- **move** it to another provider in a usable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting what was done before. Parents can do this in the portal under **Settings → Privacy & consent**. The school is told and decides what follows; withdrawing does not delete records automatically, because some must be kept by law or for the child's schooling.
- **not be subject to decisions made only by a computer** that significantly affect you. AI School OS does not make such decisions: staff review AI suggestions.

## 9. How information is protected

- Each school's data is kept separate from every other school's.
- Access by role and permission; children cannot reach other children's records.
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS); stored keys for payment, SMS and other services are encrypted.
- Two-step sign-in, which a school can require for powerful staff roles.
- An audit log of important actions, including every data export.
- Regular backups by the hosting provider.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, the school and, where the law requires, the NDPC and affected people are told.
